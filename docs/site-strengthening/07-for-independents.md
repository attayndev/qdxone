## The owner-operator hiring trap *(4 cards)*

- **You're the recruiter.** Posting the ad, reading the replies, chasing
  the no-shows. All while running prep and covering the line.
- **You're the interviewer.** 20 minutes per applicant — and the ones who
  wash out take a week of training with them.
- **You're the trainer.** A week of your time goes into someone, then they
  ghost you on a Saturday.
- **You're the cover.** When a hire washes out, who's behind the counter?
  You are.
