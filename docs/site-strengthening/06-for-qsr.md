## The restaurant hiring math *(stat cards → statement cards)*

- **20 min** — of manager time per interview, multiplied by every weak
  applicant your managers sat with this week.
- **Nonstop** — frontline turnover means hiring never really ends — and a
  bad first hire restarts the clock.
- **Twice** — the most expensive hire is the one you make twice: training
  hours, re-posting, and covered shifts, all over again.

## What changes when QDXone handles your applications

- **Manager time goes back where it belongs.** Less time sorting
  applicants, more time running the floor. The interviews your managers do
  run start from a scored shortlist, with flags worth asking about.
- **Hiring quality stops being a personality lottery.** Different
  locations, different managers, different instincts. QDXone gives every
  restaurant the same bands so the bar doesn't drift store to store.
  **[CHANGED — "the same scored signal" → "the same bands": concrete.]**
- **A consistent process you can stand behind.** The same assessment for
  everyone, plain Low/Mid/High bands instead of gut calls, and built-in
  checks that flag if any group is being screened out at a lower rate. No
  applicant is ever turned down by the system — a person makes every
  hiring decision.

- **Hire the behaviors guests notice.** Reliability, people skills,
  ownership, composure — the things that show up at the counter and on the
  floor. Hire them in instead of trying to coach them in.
