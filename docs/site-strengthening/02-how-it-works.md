## The four steps

1. **Post the role** — Pick a role you've defined (Team Member, Shift Lead,
   whatever you call them) and QDXone gives you a shareable link plus a QR
   code for your counter, window, or socials. You control the application
   fields and your own roles.
   *Detail:* Use your branded QDXone hiring page anywhere you recruit — job
   boards, social media, your website, or a QR code inside the restaurant.
2. **They apply and take a five-minute assessment** — A short, mobile-first
   application, then a five-minute assessment — plain-language statements
   rated on a 5-point scale, written at an everyday reading level. No
   login, no download. About 8 minutes total, and they can pick up where
   they left off for 72 hours.
   *Detail:* Quiet quality checks (attention items, response timing) keep
   the results trustworthy.
   **[CHANGED — "6th-grade reading level" → "everyday reading level":
   friendlier to read aloud; same meaning.]**
3. **You get a scored shortlist** — Each candidate comes back with plain
   Low / Mid / High bands across four categories — Reliability & Drive,
   People Skills, Ownership, Composure — plus motivation-screener flags
   like past attendance and tenure expectation, so you know what to ask
   about before you meet them.
   *Detail:* Bands, not black-box numbers. A person on your team makes
   every hiring decision.
   **[CHANGED — removed "an overall fit recommendation (Strong fit,
   Consider, Caution, Not recommended)" — Hard Constraint 1, and not in the
   Facts File output. Removed "Recommendations are decision support."]**
4. **Invite your top pick to interview** — Send a booking link by text or
   email — the candidate picks an open time straight from your calendar, no
   phone tag. Confirmations and reminders go out on their own, and it can
   sync with your Google Calendar.
   *Detail:* Optional. Set your interview types and availability once;
   candidates self-book from there.
   **[NOTE — "your top pick" keeps the operator as the picker; allowed.]**

- ❌ Sort an inbox by gut feel between rushes.

- ❌ Auto-reject anyone — every decision is yours.
