## Full description (4000 chars max)

QDX Operator is the companion app for QDXone — Shift-Ready Hiring for
restaurants. It puts your scored applicant shortlist in your pocket, so you
know who to call first from anywhere: the office, the floor, or between
rushes.

HOW IT WORKS

Every applicant to your QDXone hiring page completes a mobile application
and a five-minute assessment. QDXone scores the qualities that matter in
restaurant work — reliability, people skills, ownership, composure — into
plain Low / Mid / High bands, plus flags worth asking about in the
interview. Nobody is auto-rejected: the shortlist is your starting point,
and you make every hiring decision.

WITH THE APP YOU CAN

• Review your scored shortlist the moment applications come in
• Open a candidate's full report — bands, flags, and their application
• Invite your top pick to interview — candidates book a time themselves
• Record decisions on the spot
• Get a push notification when someone applies or finishes an assessment

BUILT FOR OPERATORS, NOT HR DEPARTMENTS

QDXone was built by a restaurant franchise owner who was tired of spending
interview hours on people who never made it past week two. No HR jargon, no
black-box numbers — plain language, made for the people who run the floor.

GOOD TO KNOW

QDX Operator is for restaurant operators and managers with a QDXone
account. Accounts are created at qdx.one — the app is sign-in only (email
code, Google, or Apple). Just exploring? Tap "Try the demo" on the sign-in
screen to browse a sample restaurant with anonymized candidates.
