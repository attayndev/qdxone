# QSR Assessment — Job Analysis & Validity-Generalization Basis

**Status: DRAFT SCAFFOLD for the I/O psychologist to verify, complete, and sign.**
(2026-07-31) This is the content-validity + validity-generalization (VG) foundation
that substantiates the assessment's job-relatedness under the Uniform Guidelines
(UGESP §1607.14C content validity; §1607.7 / §1607.14D validity generalization). It is
the linchpin of the "established personality and motivation research" claim (see the
assessment-validation spec §0/§4d) and is the **primary** validity argument — stronger,
today, than any local criterion study at n≈10.

## 1. Target job(s)
Primary: **QSR / fast-casual hourly frontline crew** (crew member / team member / cashier
/ line / prep) — the role the assessment is used to hire for today. Secondary (same
construct space, heavier weighting on Ownership/Composure): **shift lead / key holder**.
Anchor to O*NET **35-3023 (Fast Food & Counter Workers)** and **35-2021 (Food Prep)** for
an external task/KSAO reference the I/O can cite.

## 2. Job analysis — core tasks → worker requirements (KSAOs)
| Core task / duty | Behavioral requirement (KSAO) it demands |
|---|---|
| Show up for scheduled shifts, on time, reliably | Dependability, attendance, follow-through |
| Serve customers courteously through rushes | Customer warmth/service orientation; composure |
| Work a shared line/station with teammates | Team cooperation; low interpersonal friction |
| Follow food-safety, cash, and procedure rules | Rule-adherence; conscientiousness; integrity |
| Take correction from a shift lead and adjust | Coachability; openness to feedback |
| Keep pace and recover when the line backs up | Composure / stress tolerance; emotional stability |
| Handle spills/mistakes/complaints without being told | Initiative & ownership; internal locus of control |
| Push to get faster/better over a shift | Achievement drive |

These KSAOs are the behaviors the assessment measures — established, job-relevant
requirements of frontline QSR work, not personality typing.

| Self-Direction / proactivity / internal LOC | Initiative, performance, retention | Positive [VERIFY — thinner/older base than Big Five; anchor carefully] | Judge & Bono (2001, core self-evaluations/LOC); Thomas et al. (proactivity) |

**Honest caveat for the I/O to weigh:** post-Sackett (2022), corrected operational
validities for several of these are **more modest** than the classic 1990s figures;
frame the claim as "job-relevant, meta-analytically supported constructs," not inflated
single-number validities. Self-Direction/LOC has a thinner evidentiary base than the Big
Five facets — either anchor it to core-self-evaluations/proactivity literature or soften
its claim.

## 5. How local data relates (confirmatory only)
Per the validation spec: local **reliability** (Cronbach's α/ω on the 83-item pool,
computable now) and **directional criterion checks** (n≈10, PRELIMINARY) *monitor and
eventually confirm* this VG basis — they do not establish it and cannot change the
instrument until the spec's §9 power-derived thresholds clear. Adverse-impact monitoring
of the live instrument runs continuously and independently.

## 6. Limitations to state plainly
- This is content + VG validity, **not** local criterion validity (which n does not yet
  support). Claims must say "built on established research," not "proven to predict *your*
  hires."
- Job analysis should incorporate real SME input from operating partners (16 Handles) to
  be maximally defensible — this draft is a scaffold, not a substitute for that.
